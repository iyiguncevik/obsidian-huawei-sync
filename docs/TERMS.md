## 1. The software

Huawei Health Sync is a free, open-source Obsidian plugin. It lets you connect a Huawei ID and copy fitness data you already store in Huawei Health into Daily Notes on **your** device.

It is provided as-is for personal journaling. It is **not** a medical product.

## 2. Your Huawei account

You must have a Huawei ID and a Huawei Health account with cloud-synced data. You grant (and can revoke) Health Service Kit permissions on Huawei’s consent screen. We never ask you to share your Huawei password with us.

## 3. Acceptable use

Use the plugin only with **your own** Huawei Health data. Do not use it to access anyone else’s data or to violate Huawei’s or Obsidian’s terms.

## 4. Availability

Huawei may delay, limit, or refuse Health Service Kit access (for example scopes, history, or developer review). Sync only runs while Obsidian is open. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service.
